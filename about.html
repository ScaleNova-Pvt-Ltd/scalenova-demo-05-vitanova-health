CLINICAL GOVERNANCE Institutional Charter #2015

The Inviolable Physician Charter

VitaNova Health was founded under a legally binding constitution stipulating that no commercial stakeholder, board investor, or hospital administrator may set surgical conversion quotas, revenue minimums, or influence patient admission decisions.

Constitutional Protections

  • All surgical recommendations subject to mandatory unprompted tumor/surgical board review.
  • Full clinical audit transparency published in our annual Medical Directorate Report.
  • Right of immediate free second opinion by alternate department head for every inpatient.
