Institutional Client Mandate & Terms of Engagement

These Master Engagement Terms govern all advisory contracts, technical deliveries, and corporate representations between VitaNova Health and contracting entities.

Strict NDA
Mutual Non-Disclosure Required
100% Retained
Client IP Exclusivity Standard

1. Engagement Scope & Letter of Mandate

All deliverables, strategic advisory retainers, and technical specifications are formally governed by an executed Letter of Mandate (LoM) or Master Services Agreement (MSA). Proposals and discovery representations shall not constitute a binding operational covenant until formal signing by an authorized officer of VitaNova Health.

2. Intellectual Property & Deliverable Ownership

Upon receipt of full contract settlement, all bespoke codebases, structural drawings, proprietary analyses, and domain assets authored exclusively for the client shall vest irrevocably with the contracting client entity. Pre-existing frameworks, proprietary mathematical engines, and ScaleNova multi-tenant operational primitives remain the exclusive property of ScaleNova Systems.

3. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure Governance

Both parties agree to hold in strict institutional confidence all proprietary operational parameters, pricing sheets, trade secrets, and technological designs. Mutual non-disclosure obligations shall endure for a period of five (5) years following the completion of engagements.

4. Warranties & Limitation of Liability

Deliverables are rendered in accordance with Tier-1 industry standards. Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, punitive, or consequential commercial damages, including lost revenue or business interruption, exceeding the total contract fees remitted under the applicable statement of work in the preceding twelve-month period.

5. Dispute Resolution & Governing Law

These Terms and any dispute arising hereunder shall be construed and governed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. Any controversy shall be submitted to binding arbitration in Bengaluru, Karnataka, administered under the National Medical Commission (NMC) Guidelines & Clinical Establishments Act by a single arbitrator appointed mutually.

6. Digital Inquiry Portals & ScaleNova Security Verification

Inquiries dispatched via our web application gateway are authenticated via secure tokenized protocols. No database credentials or internal enterprise secrets are exposed in browser code. All transmissions comply with TLS 1.3 encryption.
