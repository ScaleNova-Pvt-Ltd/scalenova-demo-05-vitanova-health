Mako Robotic Arthroplasty Technical Dossier

Unlike standard mechanical cutting jigs that rely on eye estimation, Mako combines a pre-operative sub-millimeter CT scan with live optical trackers fixed to femur and tibia. The robotic arm locks into the pre-planned haptic boundary, preventing saw blade excursions beyond 0.5mm.

Clinical Benchmarks

  • Post-operative narcotic consumption reduced by 41% vs conventional TKR.
  • Quadriceps muscle activation achieved within 4 hours post-anesthesia.
  • Kinematic soft-tissue balance verified through full 0°–120° range of motion before final cementation.

TAVR Transcatheter Valve Pathway
