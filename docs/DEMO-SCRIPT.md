# Live Client Demo Script — VitaNova Health

Use this walkthrough when pitching to hospital managing directors, surgical clinic owners, or diagnostic chains:

1. **Clinical Trust & Reassurance**:
   - Open `index.html`.
   - Highlight the calm, professional medical aesthetic: Clinical Marine Teal, Hospital White, Deep Slate, and the soothing physiological pulse wave canvas reacting to mouse movement.
   - Show how the site immediately conveys clinical authority through accredited standards (NABH/JCI, 99.4% surgical success index, 0.08% infection rate in modular OTs).

2. **Super-Specialty & Physician Prominence**:
   - Navigate through `specialties.html` and `specialist-detail.html`.
   - Show the detailed physician profiles with international fellowships, verified credentials, and outpatient OPD timings.
   - Show `patient-experience.html` (cashless TPA desk) and `wellness.html` (preventive health checkup packages).

3. **High-Converting Doctor Consultation Funnel**:
   - Open `book-appointment.html`.
   - Demonstrate the structured booking fields: Specialty, Hospital Campus, In-Person vs Video Teleconsultation, Preferred Date & Slot, and Cashless Insurance selection.
   - Submit an appointment. Show the generated `SN-VIT-XXXXXX` patient appointment card.
   - Confirm that the appointment lands directly in `Demo5_Healthcare` and Frappe CRM.
