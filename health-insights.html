CLINICAL CARDIOLOGY AUDIT IRB Approved #2024-CT-09

Diagnostic Stratification in Female Acute Coronary Syndromes

Background: Female patients presenting with coronary ischemia demonstrate non-obstructive coronary artery disease (MINOCA) or microvascular spasm at a rate 3.4x higher than males, leading to critical diagnostic delays in conventional emergency triage.

Study Findings & Protocol

  • Routine implementation of high-sensitivity Troponin-I sex-specific cutoffs improved diagnostic accuracy by 26%.
  • Coronary CT angiography (CCTA) identified soft plaque erosion that was completely invisible on conventional planar fluoroscopy.
  • Revised protocol reduced emergency department discharge of active ischemic patients to absolute zero across 18 months.
