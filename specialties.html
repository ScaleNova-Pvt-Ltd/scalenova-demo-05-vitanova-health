CLINICAL EMERGENCY PROTOCOL

Primary Angioplasty (PPCI) Door-to-Balloon

For acute ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI), every minute saved preserves cardiac muscle viability. VitaNova operates a 24/7 dedicated Code STEMI activation team with an audited median door-to-balloon time of 42 minutes.

PPCI Care Milestones:
• Minute 0–5: Immediate 12-lead ECG and direct digital transmission to interventional cardiologist
• Minute 5–15: Cath lab pre-activated while patient is transferred from emergency triage
• Minute 15–30: Radial artery puncture and coronary angiography under Siemens Artis Q biplane imaging
• Minute 30–42: Culprit artery balloon dilatation and drug-eluting stent deployment with TIMI-3 flow restoration

SURGICAL ROBOTICS SPECIFICATION

Mako SmartRobotics Sub-Millimeter Arthroplasty

Conventional knee and hip replacements rely on manual cutting blocks with up to 3mm angular error. VitaNova utilizes CT-based 3D anatomical modeling paired with active robotic haptic guidance.

Technical Advantages:
• Accuracy: Angular bone resection within 0.5 degrees and 0.5mm depth tolerance
• Soft-Tissue Sparing: Robotic arm haptic boundary locks prevent accidental injury to the PCL and MCL ligaments
• Kinematic Balancing: Dynamic intra-operative assessment of ligament tension through full range of flexion (0° to 140°)
• Discharge: Over 92% of patients walk on day 1 post-op with zero pain pump dependency
