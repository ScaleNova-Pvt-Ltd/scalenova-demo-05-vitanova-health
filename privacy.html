Client Confidentiality & Privacy Policy

VitaNova Health treats all prospective client briefs, financial disclosures, and operational parameters with strict institutional discretion, complying fully with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

Zero Monetization
We Never Sell Client Data
AES-256 / TLS 1.3

India Resident
Sovereign Infrastructure Isolation

1. Data Fiduciary Commitment

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023, VitaNova Health operates as a Data Fiduciary regarding corporate contacts, appointment inquiries, and technical briefs submitted through this portal. We process personal data solely for purpose-limited evaluation of professional engagements.

2. Information We Collect

We collect identity and institutional verification data (such as Name, Corporate Email, Direct Telephone, Entity Name, and Inquiry Scope) explicitly provided via our secure interactive consultation forms. Technical telemetry (such as anonymized IP address, browser user-agent, and session timestamps) is logged strictly for anti-abuse and rate-limiting enforcement.

3. Cryptographic Storage & Zero Client-Side Secret Exposure

In accordance with ScaleNova security architecture, all form submissions are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and stored at rest using AES-256 encryption. Zero administrative credentials, API tokens, or direct database keys are exposed within the frontend JavaScript environment.

4. Cookie Governance & Session Privacy

We employ strictly necessary session cookies to maintain secure form verification and prevent CSRF attacks. Performance cookies help us monitor server response latencies. You can modify your preferences at any time using our Cookie Preferences modal accessible from the footer.

5. Data Principal Rights & Data Erasure

Contracting officers and individuals hold the right to inspect, correct, or request the irrevocable erasure of their personal inquiry data from our CRM records by transmitting a formal notice to our Data Protection Officer at care@vitanova.example.com.
