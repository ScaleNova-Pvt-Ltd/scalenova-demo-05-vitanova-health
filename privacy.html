Clinical Governance

Protected Health Information (PHI) Privacy Policy

VitaNova Health upholds strict confidentiality regarding diagnostic reports, medical histories, surgical notes, and patient consultation records in compliance with national and international health data governance frameworks.

1. Electronic Medical Record Security

All clinical summaries, DICOM imaging files, and lab results are protected under AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Access is limited strictly to treating physicians, authorized clinical nursing staff, and verified hospital pharmacists.

2. Enterprise CRM Integration

Outpatient appointments and inquiries routed through ScaleNova Enterprise Health Gateway are scrubbed of marketing identifiers and synchronized directly to hospital management systems.
