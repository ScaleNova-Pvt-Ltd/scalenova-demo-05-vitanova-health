Medical Philosophy

Evidence-Based Medicine Without Compromise

Established in 2015 by a consortium of senior cardiac and orthopedic surgeons, VitaNova Health was designed around a singular premise: clinical outcomes improve when hospitals are led by doctors rather than private-equity spreadsheets.

ETHICAL PROTOCOLS

Zero Unnecessary Surgeries

Every major surgical recommendation undergoes mandatory peer review by a multi-disciplinary tumor or surgical board to ensure conservative medical management has been thoroughly explored.

ACCREDITATION

NABH & JCI Standards

Our surgical suites, clinical laboratories, blood bank, and inpatient units operate under continuous National Accreditation Board for Hospitals and Healthcare Providers compliance.
